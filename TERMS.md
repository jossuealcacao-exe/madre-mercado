# Terms for publishing in MADRE's Market

> **DRAFT** (2026-10-28), pending review by MADRE's author. Not in force until this line is gone.

By sending a module to this repository:

1. **It is yours.** You state that you wrote the code or have the right to publish it, and that it
   infringes nobody's rights.
2. **Its license.** You publish it under the license you state in `spdx`, which must allow it to live
   beside MADRE (AGPL v3). Whoever installs it receives that license.
3. **What it declares is true.** What your module says it does (where it reaches, what it sends,
   which keys it asks for) is what it does. It collects nothing from its users without declaring it in
   `reaches`.
4. **Review and signature.** MADRE's author may accept it, ask for changes or turn it down, with no
   obligation to publish it. The signature says it was reviewed, not that it has no bugs.
5. **Taking it back.** MADRE's author may take a module or a version out of the Market at any time,
   for example if it stops working, does something it does not declare, or you ask. A module taken
   back is removed from every MADRE that has it.
6. **Responsibility.** You answer for your module. MADRE and its author do not answer for what a
   community module does.
7. **Free, for now.** Community modules are free; paid ones will come later, with terms of their own.
8. **Your name.** You agree that your name and GitHub user are shown with the module.
